## 1. What the agent comes to do

In priority order:

1. See what will enter my account and how much of it I can spend.
2. Record a negócio quickly.
3. Record a payment, including one that differs from what I expected.
4. Record expenses without repetitive work.
5. See which receipts I have already recorded.

The core journey covers tasks 1 to 3.

## 2. Conventions

- The interface is available in Portuguese, English and French (foundation, section 14; issue #9). Wireframes and quoted copy in this document are the `pt-PT` wording, which addresses the agent as "tu". English and French wording is in the glossary (section 2.2) and in the translation files.
- Selection, formatting, numeric input, untranslated fiscal terms and Portal labels follow the foundation's section 14. This document does not restate those rules. The wireframes use the `pt-PT` format, `5 000,00 €`.
- Every screen must hold its layout in all three languages. Labels are not sized to the Portuguese text.
- Mobile first. Every wireframe is a phone-width layout. Wider layouts are derived later.
- Wireframes are low fidelity. They fix information hierarchy and navigation, not visual styling.
- The wireframes exist in two forms. The text wireframes in this document are authoritative and are what is reviewed. `2026-10-04-ux-core-journey.excalidraw` is an editable companion for showing the screens to people; where the two differ, this document is right.
- Status is never conveyed by colour alone. Every status has a text label.
- The interface performs no money arithmetic. Every figure that updates as the agent types ("A tua parte", "Com este valor, entra na conta") comes from the server's calculation engine through a preview request, so there is one engine.
- A preview is not a promise. The profile, the payment schedule or a settlement can change between a preview and the confirmation. Therefore:
  - while a preview is loading, the figure shows "A calcular…" and the flow cannot be confirmed;
  - a failed preview shows "Não foi possível calcular. Tentar novamente" and the flow cannot be confirmed;
  - a response for inputs the agent has since changed is ignored;
  - at confirmation the server recalculates. If the outcome differs from what was displayed, nothing is saved, the screen shows the new figures with "Os valores foram atualizados. Revê antes de confirmar.", and the agent must review and confirm again.
- The mechanism is chosen in the implementation plan.
- A multi-step flow keeps its state in the browser and saves once, at its confirmation step.

### 3.1 Flow A: record a negócio

**Goal and entry point.** The agent wants a deal they are working on to count in their forecast. Entry: the "Novo negócio" button on Negócios and on an empty Previsão, and step 4 of onboarding.

**Wireframe W1: Novo negócio**

```text
┌──────────────────────────────────┐
│ ✕  Novo negócio                  │
├──────────────────────────────────┤
│ Nome do negócio                  │
│ [ T2 Campo de Ourique          ] │
│                                  │
│ Comissão da agência (sem IVA)    │
│ [ 10000,00                   ] € │
│                                  │
│ A tua percentagem                │
│ [ 50                         ] % │
│                                  │
│ A tua parte        5 000,00 €    │
│                                  │
│ Quando esperas receber           │
│ [ novembro 2026              ▾ ] │
│                                  │
│ Fase                             │
│ (Potencial)(Provável)(Confirmado)│
│                                  │
│ Agência: Imo Lisboa, Lda  Alterar│
│                                  │
│ [        Guardar negócio       ] │
└──────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Fields**

| Field | Default | Required | Validation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nome do negócio | Empty | Yes | Not blank |
| Comissão da agência (sem IVA) | Empty | Yes | Greater than zero |
| A tua percentagem | From the profile | Yes | Between 0 and 100 |
| Quando esperas receber | Current month | Yes | A month |
| Fase | None selected | Yes | Potencial, Provável or Confirmado |
| Agência | From the profile | No | Name; NIF only when the receipt assistant needs it |

"A tua parte" is read-only and updates as the agent types.

Amount and percentage fields follow the numeric input rules of the foundation's section 14.3: while editing they hold only digits and the decimal separator, with the symbol beside the field, and displayed figures use the full format.

**Behaviour**

- Saving creates the negócio with one payment part for the full share (product core, section 8.2) and returns to where the agent came from, with the new negócio visible.
- "Perdido" is not offered at creation. It is an action on an existing negócio.
- Splitting the payment is not on this screen. It is an action on the negócio (W3), so the common case stays short.
- ✕ with unsaved input asks "Sair sem guardar?". Nothing is created.
- Validation messages appear beside the field, in words: "Indica a comissão da agência."

**States**

- Saving: the button shows "A guardar…" and is disabled.
- Error on save: the form stays filled, with "Não foi possível guardar. Tenta novamente."
- Read-only phase: the entry points are replaced by the renewal prompt (foundation, section 7.2).

**Accessibility**

- Every input has a visible label, not a placeholder.
- Fase is a group of radio buttons with the group label "Fase", operable by keyboard.
- Errors are announced and linked to their field.

**Acceptance criteria**

- An agent records a negócio with four inputs in under 60 seconds.
- After saving, the agent can say what their share is and in which month it counts.

**Evidence**

- Untested. Open question: do agents think of the commission as the agency's amount without IVA, or as some other figure (with IVA, or their own share)?

**Goal and entry point.** The agent wants to know what will enter their account and how much of it they can spend. Entry: the home screen.

```text
├──────────────────────────────────┤

│ │ cenário Confirmado.          │ │
│ │ Não é o saldo da tua conta.  │ │
│ └──────────────────────────────┘ │
│ Comissão            5 000,00 €   │
│   Já recebido           0,00 €   │
│   Por receber       5 000,00 €   │
│ Entrará na conta    4 900,00 €   │
│ Dinheiro a proteger 1 900,00 € ▾ │
│   Reserva de IVA    1 150,00 €   │
│   Reserva de IRS      250,00 €   │
│   Seg. Social (est.)  500,00 €   │
│ Despesas              350,00 €   │
│                                  │
│ Como se chega a este valor  ›    │
│                                  │
│ Próximos meses                   │
│ dez 2026   É teu      −350,00 €  │
│ jan 2027   É teu      −350,00 €  │
│ …                                │
├──────────────────────────────────┤
│ Previsão Negócios Despesas Recib.│
└──────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Fields.** None. The scenario and the month are selections.

**Behaviour**

- The four amounts are those of the product core, section 5.5, with its labels. "Entrará na conta" and "É realmente teu" are always shown as separate lines with separate labels, including when their values coincide.
- "É realmente teu" is the first and largest figure, because it answers the agent's question. This hierarchy is a hypothesis to test, not a settled decision.
- The headline is always qualified in place: it names the month and the selected scenario, and states "Não é o saldo da tua conta." It can include commissions that are expected, or only potential, so it must never read as money already available.
- The month separates what was already received from what is still expected ("Já recebido", "Por receber"). In the Provável and Total scenarios the expected part also names the stages it includes.
- "Dinheiro a proteger" expands to its components. The Segurança Social reserve always carries "(est.)".
- "Como se chega a este valor" opens a plain-language walk through the month: what enters, what is set aside and why, what is spent, what is left. It shows figures and never formulas.
- The scenario switch explains itself once: "Confirmado: só negócios confirmados e valores já recebidos." No probabilities are shown.
- Tapping a month in "Próximos meses" selects it. Tapping a line opens the negócios or expenses behind it.
- A negative "É realmente teu" is shown with a minus sign and the text "Neste mês as despesas e reservas ultrapassam o que entra."
- An overdue payment appears in the current month with "Em atraso desde <mês>" (product core, section 7.4).

**States**

- Empty (no negócio yet): "Ainda não tens negócios. Regista o primeiro para veres quanto vais receber." with the "Novo negócio" button.
- Loading: the layout with placeholders, no spinner replacing the page.
- Error: "Não foi possível carregar a previsão." with "Tentar novamente".
- Read-only phase: figures visible, with a banner "A tua subscrição terminou. Renova para voltares a editar." (foundation, section 7.2).
- Past due: a banner "Pagamento pendente" with "Atualizar método de pagamento" (foundation, section 7.1).

**Accessibility**

- Each amount is a label and value pair read together by a screen reader.
- Negative values are announced as negative, not conveyed by colour.
- The scenario switch and month navigation are keyboard operable, and the change of month is announced.

**Acceptance criteria**

- Asked "how much could you spend from what you receive in November?", the agent answers with the "É realmente teu" figure, not the "Entrará na conta" figure.
- The agent can say, in their own words, why the two figures differ.
- The agent can find which negócios make up a month.
- Asked whether the headline figure is in their bank account today, the agent says no, and can say which part of the month is already received.

**Evidence**

- Untested. Open questions: do agents understand "Dinheiro a proteger"? Do they read "É realmente teu" as spendable money? Do they mistake it for their current bank balance? Is a conservative IVA reserve understood by agents who deduct IVA?

**Behaviour**

- The authoritative input is the commission without IVA (product core, section 8.3). W4 shows the prefilled commission and the calculated bank inflow side by side, and the agent confirms the commission explicitly. A bank amount that matches is not treated as proof that the commission and the fiscal settings are right.
- The common case needs no typing: the first answer confirms both figures as shown.
- The second answer keeps the commission and records a different observed bank amount. "Valor que entrou na conta" never drives the calculation. When it differs from the calculated inflow, W6 shows calculated, observed and difference, and the agent ticks "Confirmo esta diferença" before confirming.
- No answer is preselected in W4.
- W5 appears only when the receipt or correction leaves a residual. Its heading, options and order follow the sign of the residual calculated for the whole negócio (product core, section 6.3):
  - positive residual: "Ainda falta receber", "A comissão foi menor", or correcting the negócio;
  - negative residual: "Antecipa valores em falta" (offered only up to the outstanding balance), "A comissão foi maior", or correcting the negócio.
- Each option states its server-calculated effect on the whole negócio (received, outstanding, effective commission) before the agent chooses.
- No option is preselected in W5. The agent must choose.
- The third option opens W5c inside the flow. The agent edits the agency commission, the percentage, or both. The resulting share and the balance of the whole negócio are previewed by the server, under the preview rules of section 2.
- "‹" on W5c returns to W5 with nothing changed. "Continuar" goes to W6 when the previewed residual is zero. When a residual remains, it returns to W5, which now shows that remaining residual and its options.
- The source edits are not saved on W5c. They are saved only at W6, together with the settlement, in one confirmation (product core, section 6.3).
- When source values were edited, W6 lists them before the payment: "Dados do negócio corrigidos: Comissão da agência 10 000,00 € → 9 000,00 €; A tua parte 5 000,00 € → 4 500,00 €".
- W6 is the confirmation boundary. Nothing is saved before "Confirmar recebimento". ✕ or "Voltar" at any earlier point leaves the negócio untouched.
- After confirming, the agent returns to the negócio, which shows the part as "Recebido a 14/11/2026" with "Corrigir recebimento", the new December part when one was created, and the adjustment and effective commission when an adjustment was recorded (W3b). A message says "Recebimento registado. A previsão foi atualizada."
- When the negócio is not Confirmado, the button on W3 reads "Confirmar negócio e registar recebimento" and W6 states "O negócio passa a Confirmado." The stage never changes silently.
- "Corrigir recebimento" reopens this flow with the current values. Its confirmation text is "A correção substitui o registo anterior, que fica guardado."
- A correction never reuses "received less" or "received more" wording. A corrected amount can still be below the original expectation and yet leave a negative residual, as in the second W5 example.
- A correction that changes only the date shows no W5: the earlier resolution is kept (product core, section 6.3).

**States**

- Submitting: "A registar…", button disabled. A repeated tap does not create a second record (product core, section 9.4).
- Rejected: when the server answers that the receipt was not recorded, W6 stays as it was, with "Não foi possível registar o recebimento. Nada foi alterado." and the reason.
- Unknown outcome: when no answer arrives (a timeout or a lost connection), the receipt may have been recorded. The screen says "Não foi possível confirmar se o recebimento ficou registado." with "Verificar novamente". That action repeats exactly the submitted payload with the same idempotency key, so it either returns the record already created or creates it once. The screen never claims that nothing changed.
  - The submitted payload is immutable during recovery. The fields are shown, locked, with the values that were sent.
  - No edit and no refreshed preview value is ever sent under that key.
  - The original attempt must be resolved first. Only once its outcome is known does the screen offer what comes next: "Corrigir recebimento" when it was recorded, or a new submission, under a new key, when it was not.
- Updated figures: when the server's recalculation differs from what was displayed, see the preview rule in section 2.
- Read-only phase: "Registar recebimento" is replaced by the renewal prompt.
- Archived negócio: an option that would leave an amount outstanding explains "Este negócio está arquivado. Desarquiva-o para registar um valor por receber."

**Accessibility**

- The choices in W4 and W5 are radio groups with the question as the group label.
- The calculated inflow is announced when it changes as the agent types.
- W6 is reachable and confirmable by keyboard alone, and focus moves to the success message after confirming.

**Acceptance criteria**

- An agent paid the expected amount records it without retyping either amount, with an explicit final confirmation, and can say what commission they confirmed.
- An agent whose bank amount differs while the commission is as expected can record that without changing the commission.
- An agent correcting 900,00 € to 950,00 € with 100,00 € outstanding sees the outstanding amount fall to 50,00 €.
- After a lost connection at confirmation, the agent ends with exactly one settlement.
- After choosing "A comissão foi menor", the agent finds the derived share, the adjustment and the effective commission on the negócio.
- An agent who corrects the negócio's source values sees them listed on W6, and finds them unchanged if they leave before confirming.
- An agent paid less can say, before confirming, what will happen to the difference.
- After confirming, the agent finds the payment in the right month of the forecast.
- An agent who cancels at any step before W6's confirmation finds nothing changed.

**Evidence**

- Untested. This flow carries the largest comprehension risk in the product:
  - agents know what landed in their bank, not their commission without IVA, so "Valor da tua comissão (sem IVA)" may be the wrong thing to ask first;
  - "Ainda falta receber" versus "A comissão foi menor" may not match how agents think about a short payment;
  - the meaning of "ficam fixos" may be unclear.

## 5. Prototype

- A small standalone Vue prototype, separate from the production code, with fixed synthetic fixtures.
- It implements no calculation. Every figure comes from the fixtures, which were computed once by the rules in the product core.

**Entry.** One plain screen, not a marketing page: the discreet API Portugal note and the two choices. **Common rules.**

- Fictitious data is acceptable; contradictory results are not.
- No field looks free while accepting only undisclosed values. Prepared data is shown as identified example data.
- Every visible action is supported. Nothing ends in "Fora deste protótipo", and no disabled save buttons are shown.
- No second financial engine: every figure is a prepared literal.
- Issuing on the Portal and reading a PDF are simulations. No real document is requested and nothing leads to a real issuance.
- The moderator's guide and expected answers stay outside these demonstrations.
- Progress survives a reload, and so does the place: the screen of the current step or point is restored, without executing any operation. The stored state is kept per demonstration and carries a data version; a state saved by an older version is discarded instead of loaded.

**The guide and the application are separate.** The person must tell at once which is which: "this is Agentimo; this is the explanation that accompanies me." A different colour on a band is not enough.

- **The guide sits outside the application area.** It is labelled "Guia da demonstração · não faz parte da aplicação" and holds the step, the orientation and the navigation. On a wide screen it is a separate side panel. On a phone it is a compact band above the application that expands.
- **Inside the application there is only what the agent sees:** negócios, forecast, expenses, receipts and their buttons. Ordinary messages such as "Despesa guardada" belong to the application. Explanations such as "what changed", "in this example", "we filled this in for you" and "consultation only" belong to the guide.
- **Prepared data appears in the real controls.** Recording a negócio shows the form the agent will use, already filled in. The guide says the data was filled in for this visit. The fields are locked, so no edit can produce an unsupported result, and touching a locked field makes the guide say so. A list of values with a "Guardar" button is not used: it does not show what recording a negócio is like.
- **A demonstration bar is always visible, outside the application,** even with the guide hidden and while the page is scrolled: it stays at the top of the screen and never covers a focused field. It is labelled "Demonstração · dados preparados" and carries three controls that never disappear: show or hide the guide, "Mudar de demonstração" and "Recomeçar". It also shows the short notices that must never be hidden: that fields are prepared and locked, and that an operation is simulated. Hiding the guide never hides the simulated nature of an operation: selecting "Carregar o PDF do recibo" with the guide hidden still shows "Leitura do PDF simulada: nenhum ficheiro foi pedido nem lido."
- **"Ocultar guia"** hides the orientation and leaves the application and the demonstration bar.
- **The guide can always be collapsed.** The person's choice prevails over any automatic opening. Collapsed, it shows only the current instruction. The closing questions and the general explanations appear at the end or when the person expands the guide.
- **The guide only explains the screen that is open.** When the person leaves the destination of the current step or point, the guide stops showing that explanation and says "Estás a explorar livremente", with "Retomar visita". It never keeps a financial explanation that no longer matches the screen. This applies to the guided journey too: it takes place in November, so opening another month of the forecast or of the expenses counts as leaving the step.
- **Restarting brings the guide back.** "Recomeçar" restores the first step with the guide shown and collapsed, even when the guide was hidden.
- **The simulated Portal belongs to the guide.** "Abrir o Portal das Finanças" in the application opens a screen of the guide, outside the application area, labelled "Simulação: isto não é o Portal das Finanças".
- **The final review belongs to the guide.** At the last step the application shows the forecast, and the guide shows what was done, the account of the month and the closing questions.
- **On a phone** the guide stays compact, with a one-line "O que mudou" after an operation, so that scenario, month and main amount remain visible without scrolling and a form is never pushed below the first screen. It opens by itself only at the last step, where the review is the content of the step, and can be collapsed there too.

### 5.5 Verification before the demonstrations are made available

- **Transitions.** Going back without duplication; skipping without invented results or unlocked actions; the final review stating only what was done.
- **Restart and switching.** Cancel preserves, confirm restores the start. Switching keeps the two states apart, also after a reload. A reload restores the screen of the current step, including the final review at step 7. A stored state of another data version is discarded.
- **Copy.** The effective clipboard content, on the address used to open the demonstration. "Copiado" refers only to the value copied on the sheet that is open: it is cleared when the sheet or the demonstration changes.
- **Phone.** Scenario, month and main amount visible without scrolling, at 390×844 and 360×640, with the guide compact.

### 5.6 Commenting on a screen

Tracking: [issue #12](https://github.com/CrmWare/propware-agentimo/issues/12).

A person exploring a demonstration can send an opinion about the screen they are looking at, without leaving the visit.

- **Where.** "Comentar este ecrã" is a control of the demonstration bar, outside the application. Nothing about comments appears inside the application.
- **Never imposed.** The window never opens by itself and feedback is never required.
- **What is asked.** One text field and "Enviar opinião". No account, name or email.
- **Context.** Captured when the window opens and shown in it: demonstration, step or point of the guide, screen and what is open in it (the negócio, the month and scenario, the sub-screen of expenses or receipts). Free exploration is distinguished from a step actually accompanied by the guide. The context sent is exactly the one shown.
- **Notice.** "Enviamos este texto e o contexto acima. Não pedimos o seu nome nem o seu email. O alojamento regista dados técnicos do pedido, como o endereço IP, durante um período limitado. O rascunho perde-se se recarregar a página." The window does not claim anonymity.
- **Result.** "Opinião enviada. Obrigado." only after the service's own answer confirms that the comment is stored; any other answer is not a success. A refusal keeps the text, editable, and offers another attempt. When the outcome is unknown, the text is locked and the same request is repeated; a refusal of a repeated request does not unlock it. The person can start a new comment instead, and is told first: "O comentário anterior pode já ter sido recebido. Continuar inicia um novo comentário."
- **Limit.** Up to 2 000 characters, stated next to the field with the count written so far.
- **Drafts.** A draft exists only in memory and stays attached to the screen it was written for. Reopening the window elsewhere says so and offers to move the draft to the current screen; it is never moved silently.
- **No effect on the visit.** Opening, writing, sending and closing changes no negócio, expense, payment, receipt, month, scenario or progress, and nothing about a comment is stored in the browser.
- **Screen level only.** No element selection, screenshots, session recording, ratings or behavioural tracking.
- **Where comments go.** One file per comment in a private store of the demonstration's hosting, readable only by the team that runs it and not reachable over the web. The file holds the text, the context and the time of receipt. No field identifying the visitor is asked for or added; the free text may contain whatever the person chooses to write. No file is kept more than 90 days: the product owner reviews the store every 30 days and deletes what is older than 60 days, and deletes everything when the demonstration is withdrawn.
- **Accessibility.** The window is a modal dialog with an accessible name; focus moves into the text field on opening and returns to the control on closing; it can be used by keyboard alone and on a phone.

## 7. Hypothesis: property photo on a negócio

**Status: proposed, not approved for the MVP.** This section is a UX hypothesis and a feasibility investigation. It is not a committed launch feature. Nothing in the product core or the foundation is amended for it.

### 7.1 Two separate questions

1. **Recognition.** Does a property photo help an agent identify the right negócio more easily than text alone? This is the stronger hypothesis. Saving typing is not: the title is one short field.
2. **Source.** If a photo helps, how is it obtained? The proposed first integration is an Idealista listing import. A manual upload is the alternative.

Question 1 is tested first. No integration is committed to before a photo has shown its value. The two are kept apart in testing: the recognition test says nothing about how the photo arrives.

**Design of the test.** The test must not favour the photo by construction.

- **Realistic names.** Negócios are named as agents would name them, with the information agents normally have: a street or area, the type of property, and sometimes the agency's reference. Names are not made deliberately confusing.
- **Two kinds of task,** because each kind of cue suits one version better:
  - *visually prompted*: the participant is shown a photo of the property, as they would remember it from a visit, and asked to open its negócio;
  - *reference or location based*: the participant is given what they would have in a message or a call, for example "the flat in Avenida de Roma" or "reference 2841", and asked to open its negócio.
- **Different property sets.** Fixtures D1 and D2 are two different sets of eight properties, matched for the mix of types and locations. A participant uses one set with the text-only list and the other with the photo list, so no property is seen twice.
- **Counterbalancing.** Which set is paired with which version, and which version comes first, alternate between participants.
- **Measures.** For each task: whether the correct negócio was selected, the time to select it, and the number of wrong negócios opened first. Stated preference is noted but is not the measure.
- **Reporting.** Results are reported separately for the two kinds of task. A photo list that helps only when the prompt is itself a photo is a weaker result than one that helps in both.
- **Limits.** With 3 to 5 agents the results are exploratory. They can suggest whether a photo helps recognition; they are not proof that one version is better, and they say nothing about usefulness or willingness to pay.
- The photos are synthetic or authorised for reuse. No real listing is used.

### 7.3 Proposed source: Idealista listing import

**Proposed interaction**

- "Novo negócio" offers an optional Idealista listing URL.
- Agentimo attempts to suggest a short title and one cover photo.
- The agent reviews the suggestion before accepting it. Existing input is never overwritten without confirmation.
- Manual creation remains available. An import failure never blocks saving.
- No commission, agent share or fiscal setting is inferred from the listing.

**Scope limits**

- One-time import only.
- No listing monitoring, synchronisation, property catalogue or automatic price updates.

**Implementation gate.** Before any implementation, confirm with Idealista:

- authorised access to Portuguese listings, and lookup by URL or listing identifier;
- permission to display and, if needed, store the title and the photo;
- attribution, retention and removal requirements;
- access costs and usage limits.

An authorised integration is used. A publicly accessible listing is not assumed to permit scraping or reuse. If access or permission is unavailable, automatic import stays out of the MVP.

**Security and privacy**

- Any server-side fetch restricts its destinations, validates redirects and enforces response-size and timeout limits.
- Stored images follow Agentimo's ownership, access and erasure rules.

**In the prototype,** import behaviour is simulated with synthetic listing data and is marked as simulated.

### 7.4 Alternatives and their limits

- **A stored link alone** does not test photo recognition. It shows no image.
- **Manual photo upload** avoids automated fetching, but it is not a zero-cost fallback. It still needs image rights, secure processing, storage and erasure.

**Behaviour**

- The total of the month on D1 is the same figure the forecast shows as "Despesas" for that month, and it separates what is paid from what is expected.
- The amount is the gross cash amount. The label says "com IVA" so the agent does not enter a net figure.
- A recurring expense is entered once. Its monthly occurrences appear by themselves and are never stored until confirmed.
- "Confirmar pagamento" is a confirmation boundary: it shows the real amount (prefilled with the expected one) and the payment date (prefilled with today), both editable, and writes the paid record only when the agent confirms them.
- The payment date decides the month the expense belongs to (product core, section 7.2). The screen says so when the date falls in a different month from the expected one: "Com esta data, a despesa passa para <mês>."
- A past month whose recurring occurrences were never confirmed is shown as "Por rever". Until reviewed, those occurrences count at their expected amount and are labelled as an estimate. They are never shown as paid.
- D3 offers, per occurrence: confirm, correct the amount, or "Não houve" (skip); and one action to confirm all unchanged. Only a confirmation writes a paid record.
- "Confirmar todas sem alterar" confirms the amounts, never the dates by itself. It opens a confirmation step that lists each occurrence with its amount and a payment date field. No date is assumed: the agent enters each date, or enters one and applies it to all with "Usar esta data em todas". Nothing is written until every listed occurrence has a date and the agent confirms the list. An occurrence the agent cannot date is left unreviewed. Agentimo never invents a payment date.
- A paid expense is corrected by "Corrigir pagamento", which replaces the record and keeps the original. An expected expense is edited freely.
- An expense linked to a negócio stays counted if the negócio becomes perdido. D1 says so on that expense: "Negócio perdido · a despesa mantém-se".
- An expected one-off expense whose date has passed is shown as "Em atraso desde <mês>" and counted in the current month, once.

**States**

- Empty: "Ainda não tens despesas. Regista as que se repetem todos os meses para a previsão ficar certa." with "Nova despesa".
- Read-only phase: the list is visible; adding, confirming and correcting are replaced by the renewal prompt.
- Errors, unknown outcomes and previews follow the rules of sections 2 and 3.3.

**Accessibility**

- Paid, expected, estimate and overdue are text labels, never colour alone.
- The three actions per occurrence in D3 are a labelled group per expense.

**Acceptance criteria**

- An agent enters a monthly cost once and finds it in every later month of the forecast.
- An agent can say which of last month's costs are confirmed and which are estimates.
- After confirming a payment with a different amount and a date in the expected month, that month's total and its "É realmente teu" change by the same difference, and no other month changes.
- After confirming a payment with a date in another month, the expense leaves the month where it was expected and appears in the month of its date; both months change, and the agent can say why.
- After "Confirmar todas sem alterar", every confirmed occurrence has a date the agent entered or explicitly applied.

**Evidence**

- Untested. Open questions: do agents think of costs with or without IVA? Is "Por rever" understood, or ignored? Is reviewing a past month too much effort to be done?

**In the prototype.** The expenses shown are the records of section 5.3 (six months, for consultation) and the single prepared expense of section 5.4 (guided journey). Reviewing a past month and confirming occurrences in a batch are specified here and are not part of those demonstrations.
